\section{Development Methodology}

This is primarily a research project with a large amount of code required, the development is split into two phases. 

As described in the timeline in the earlier progress report the software development was focused towards the first half of the project timeline. 
Scope was known from the research phase, challenges were identified early and goals were set in advance.
This is most closely resembling a traditional waterfall development cycle, with periods of lots of development and then acceptance testing (in my case reproducing behaviour exhibited in various research papers) towards the end of the cycle.

\vspace{1em}

The second half of the project is to utilise my code and knowledge around working with networks and the problem space to idenitify a new or novel solution to the network influence maximization problem. 
This follows more closely to a typical agile development methodology, implementing and quickly testing lots of different ideas to see if they are worthwhile and constantly changing the requirements based on what works so far and what doesn't.
